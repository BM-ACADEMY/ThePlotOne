# THE PLOT ONE

## Logo & Brand Identity — Design Brief

*ABM Groups · Pondicherry, Tamil Nadu*

**Brand name:** THE PLOT ONE **Tagline:** *Find your One*

---

## 1\. WHAT IT IS

The Plot One is a real estate platform based in Pondicherry, expanding across Tamil Nadu.

We run marketing campaigns for property sellers, generate genuine buyers for their projects, and connect those buyers to property agents who close the deals.

**We are not a listing website. We create the demand.**

### We handle every property type

|  |  |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Plots and land** | DTCP-approved layouts, individual plots |
| **Houses and villas** | Independent houses, apartments, villas |
| **Farmland** | Agricultural land, farm plots |
| **Commercial** | Shops, offices, godowns, commercial land |

Plots and layouts are our largest category today, but the brand must comfortably represent **all four**. Nothing in the mark should limit us to land only.

---

## 2\. WHO SEES THIS BRAND

**Promoters, builders and property owners** Men aged 35–60 — layout promoters, house and villa builders, commercial developers and individual owners across Pondicherry, Villianur, Bahour, Tindivanam and Tamil Nadu. They pay us ₹10,000–₹40,000 a month. They need to see a company that looks established and safe to pay.

**Property agents and brokers** Aged 25–50, working locally, almost entirely on their phones. They subscribe to receive buyers.

**Property buyers** Aged 28–50, buying plots, houses, farmland or commercial property worth ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore — often the biggest purchase of their lives. Salaried people and investors, many from nearby towns.

Mostly Tamil-speaking. Small-town and semi-urban rather than metro.

---

## 3\. WHAT THE LOGO HAS TO DO

Make a stranger trust us in the first two seconds — usually on a phone screen, often before they've read a single word about us.

---

## 4\. THE NAME AND TAGLINE

**The Plot One** — "plot" means land, but it also means *the piece of ground that is yours* — whatever stands on it. The name means *the one that's right for you*. It also reads as plot number one, the way every layout is surveyed and numbered.

**Find your One** — the promise to the buyer, whether they are looking for a plot, a house, farmland or a shop. One right property, found for you. It also carries our core difference for agents: one buyer, one agent, never shared.

**The tagline carries the breadth the name doesn't.** Keep it visible in the primary lockup — it is what tells people we're not plots-only.

The tagline should sit with the logo as a lockup, and also work separately. It must remain legible when the logo is used small.

## 6\. THE FEEL

Trustworthy · established · professional · grounded.

Someone should assume we've been doing this for ten years.

**Not** a startup look. **Not** luxury. **Not** playful.

---

## 7\. VISUAL DIRECTIONS WORTH EXPLORING

The strongest direction is something **abstract and geometric** that works across all four property types, rather than a picture of any one of them.

- Plot boundary lines or a survey grid — the geometry of land seen from above  
- One highlighted square or parcel among many — "this one"  
- Boundary, corner marker, survey pin, location marker  
- The numeral **1** integrated with a plot or parcel shape  
- A simple frame or enclosure — land, building or field can all live inside it

**Test any concept against all four:** would this mark look right beside a photo of an empty layout, a villa, a paddy field and a commercial shop? If it only suits one, it's too narrow.

---

## 8\. WHAT TO AVOID

A literal house or roof icon · keys · handshakes · skylines and tall buildings · anything that reads as "apartments only" or "land only" · generic property clip-art.

**The reason:** we sell plots, houses, farmland and commercial property. A roof outline says houses. Bare survey lines say land only. **We need a mark that covers all of it.**

---

## 9\. COLOUR

Earth and trust. Deep green or terracotta with a neutral base, and one strong accent for buttons and highlights.

Must work on white, over photographs and video, and in a single colour on a printed card.

---

## 10\. TYPOGRAPHY

Clear, modern sans-serif. Legible at small sizes. Readable to someone who isn't fluent in English.

**Tamil consideration:** some collateral will carry Tamil text. The mark should sit comfortably next to Tamil script — avoid anything that only works locked to English letterforms.

---

## 11\. DELIVERABLES

- Primary logo — horizontal, with tagline  
- Primary logo — without tagline  
- Stacked version for square placements  
- **Icon only** — profile picture use, legible at 40 × 40 px  
- Single-colour black and white versions  
- Files: **SVG** and **PNG** (transparent background)  
- Colour codes: HEX, RGB, CMYK  
- Font names and weights used

---

## 12\. ONE LINE TO KEEP IN MIND
