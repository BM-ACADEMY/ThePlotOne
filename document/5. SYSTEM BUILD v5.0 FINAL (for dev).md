# PART 1 — THE RULE THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING

> **Whoever paid for the lead owns it.**

| Lead type | Identified by | Can be matched to |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Campaign lead** | Has `campaignId` | **That campaign's project ONLY** |
| **Organic lead** | No `campaignId` | Our inventory → promoters → agents |

**Enforce in code, not policy.** A lead with a `campaignId` must be structurally incapable of matching any other inventory. If a promoter discovers his paid campaign produced a buyer who was shown our own property, the business is finished in that town.

**Campaign leads reach agents only when the promoter explicitly opts in** (`project.releaseToAgents`, default **OFF**, requested as a service). Never automatic.

# PART 2 — LEAD JOURNEY

Lead arrives (ad / reel / website / call / walk-in / requirement post)

   ↓  seconds

CREATED IN OUR DATABASE  — stored, deduped, scored, source-tagged

   ↓

Has campaignId?

   ├─ YES → deliver to THAT promoter within 60s (WhatsApp). END.

   │         telecaller qualifies in parallel, notes appended

   └─ NO  → organic routing:

             ↓

        Any in-house listing matching area \+ budget \+ type?

           ├─ NO  → skip the hold entirely, route onward NOW

           └─ YES → HOLD: Tier 1 \= 24h, Tier 2 \= 6h

                     telecaller pitches our listing

                       ├─ interested → ours, 2-3% brokerage

                       └─ not interested / no answer

                            → RELEASE IMMEDIATELY (never wait out the clock)

             ↓

        Matches an ACTIVE CAMPAIGN?

           different promoters → highest plan tier → most unsold → rotation

           SAME promoter, several campaigns →

                1\. better fit (budget band \+ area accuracy)

                2\. THE CAMPAIGN BEHIND PACE  ← self-balancing

                3\. closer to expiry

                4\. delivered ONCE, tagged to one campaign, never double-counted

             ↓ no match

        AGENT LADDER

           Tier 2: ₹3,999 → ₹1,999 → ₹999, 15 min each, one at a time

           Tier 1: open offer to 3-5 agents, 4h, phone masked

             ↓ nobody

        TELECALLER in-house — our own brokerage

**Subscriber priority:** if an organic lead matches both our own listing and a paying promoter's layout in the same area, **the paying promoter wins**. Subscription revenue is recurring; a single 2% is not.

**Tier 2 holds for 6 hours, not 24\.** A ready buyer held for a day calls someone else.

# PART 4 — COST OPTIMISATION (build these in from day one)

Messaging and infrastructure costs scale with users. Decisions that cost nothing now save lakhs later.

## 4.1 Channel routing — push first, WhatsApp only where needed

| Message type | Channel | Reason |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Lead offers to agents | **Push \+ in-app** | Free, instant, no Meta approval |
| Threshold and quota alerts | **Push \+ in-app** | Free; each carries one action button |
| Deadline and action reminders | **Push** | Free |
| Performance, score, ranking | **In-app only** | Nobody needs a message for this |
| Requirement board activity | **Push** | Free |
| Nurture and re-engagement | **Push first**, WhatsApp only if push dead | Marketing templates cost the most |
| **Lead delivery to promoters** | **WhatsApp** | They will not install an app. Non-negotiable. |
| OTP | **SMS or WhatsApp** | Required |
| Renewal and payment | **WhatsApp** | Money messages need the reliable channel |
| Buyer visit reminders | **WhatsApp** | Buyers have no app |

**Rule: if the recipient has our app installed, default to push.** Agents and staff → push. Promoters and buyers → WhatsApp.

Rough saving at 50 agents: several thousand rupees a month at launch, and it compounds with every city.

## 4.2 Other cost decisions

- **PWA, not native apps.** One codebase, no store fees, no review delays. Native only if retention proves it's needed.  
- **Free push (FCM).** No per-message cost, ever.  
- **Batch non-urgent WhatsApp** into a daily digest rather than per-event sends.  
- **Cloudinary or similar free tier** for media; compress on upload — video storage becomes the biggest infra cost.  
- **One shared telecaller team across all cities.** The single biggest cost advantage in the model; it does not degrade with distance.  
- **City partners on revenue share, not salary.** No fixed cost before revenue.  
- **Local videographers per shoot**, not employed. Zero cost until booked.  
- **Start on one modest server.** This platform will not strain infrastructure at 500 users.

| :---- | :---- | :---- |

**The four survival mechanisms:** job watchdog (15 min) · dead-letter queue · orphan detector (daily) · **7 AM self-audit — if the message doesn't arrive, that absence is the alert.**
