# THE PLOT ONE

## Logo & Brand Identity — Design Brief

*ABM Groups · Pondicherry, Tamil Nadu*

**Brand name:** THE PLOT ONE **Tagline:** *Find your One*

---

## 1\. WHAT IT IS

The Plot One is a real estate platform based in Pondicherry, expanding across Tamil Nadu.

We run marketing campaigns for layout promoters and plot developers, generate genuine buyers for their projects, and connect those buyers to property agents who close the deals.

**We are not a listing website. We create the demand.**

---

## 2\. WHO SEES THIS BRAND

**Layout promoters and plot developers** Men aged 35–60 running DTCP-approved layouts around Pondicherry, Villianur, Bahour, Tindivanam and across Tamil Nadu. They pay us ₹10,000–₹40,000 a month. They need to see a company that looks established and safe to pay.

**Property agents and brokers** Aged 25–50, working locally, almost entirely on their phones. They subscribe to receive buyers.

**Property buyers** Aged 28–50, buying plots worth ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore — often the biggest purchase of their lives. Salaried people and investors, many from nearby towns.

Mostly Tamil-speaking. Small-town and semi-urban rather than metro.

---

## 3\. WHAT THE LOGO HAS TO DO

Make a stranger trust us in the first two seconds — usually on a phone screen, often before they've read a single word about us.

---

## 4\. THE NAME AND TAGLINE

**The Plot One** — "plot" means land. The name means *the one plot that's right for you*. It also reads as plot number one, which is how every layout is surveyed and numbered.

**Find your One** — the promise to the buyer. One right plot, found for you. It also carries our core difference for agents: one buyer, one agent, never shared.

The tagline should sit with the logo as a lockup, and also work separately. It must remain legible when the logo is used small.

---

## 5\. WHERE IT WILL BE USED

| Digital | Print & physical |
| :---- | :---- |
| Instagram profile picture (small circle) | Visiting cards |
| Watermark on reels and property videos | Layout site signboards |
| Website header | Printed rate cards and proposals |
| WhatsApp Business display picture | Property brochures |
| Agent app and dashboard | Banners at property events |
| Email signature | Office signage |

**The two hardest tests:**

1. Recognisable as a tiny circle on WhatsApp (40 × 40 px)  
2. Readable on a signboard from twenty feet away

---

## 6\. THE FEEL

Trustworthy · established · professional · grounded.

Someone should assume we've been doing this for ten years.

**Not** a startup look. **Not** luxury. **Not** playful.

---

## 7\. VISUAL DIRECTIONS WORTH EXPLORING

- Plot boundary lines or a survey grid — the geometry of a layout seen from above  
- One highlighted plot among many  
- Land, boundary, corner marker, survey pin  
- The numeral **1** integrated with a plot shape  
- A pin or marker that reads as "this one"

---

## 8\. WHAT TO AVOID

House shapes · roofs · keys · handshakes · tall buildings · skylines.

**We sell land, not houses.** Most of our inventory is empty plots in approved layouts.

---

## 9\. COLOUR

Earth and trust. Deep green or terracotta with a neutral base, and one strong accent for buttons and highlights.

Must work on white, over photographs and video, and in a single colour on a printed card.

---

## 10\. TYPOGRAPHY

Clear, modern sans-serif. Legible at small sizes. Readable to someone who isn't fluent in English.

**Tamil consideration:** some collateral will carry Tamil text. The mark should sit comfortably next to Tamil script — avoid anything that only works locked to English letterforms.

---

## 11\. DELIVERABLES

- Primary logo — horizontal, with tagline  
- Primary logo — without tagline  
- Stacked version for square placements  
- **Icon only** — profile picture use, legible at 40 × 40 px  
- Single-colour black and white versions  
- Files: **SVG** and **PNG** (transparent background)  
- Colour codes: HEX, RGB, CMYK  
- Font names and weights used

---

## 12\. ONE LINE TO KEEP IN MIND

> We find the right buyer for the right plot. The mark should feel like **precision and land** — not property glamour.
