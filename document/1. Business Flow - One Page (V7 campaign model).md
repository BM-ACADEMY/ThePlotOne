## THE ONE-LINE MODEL

> **Promoters pay us to create buyers. Agents pay us to access those buyers. The same lead earns four times.**

**The unit is a CAMPAIGN, not an account.** One plan \= one project \= one guaranteed lead count. Every other project stays listed free.

> *"List everything free. Advertise what you want to sell now."*

---

## THE MONEY FLOW

PROMOTER pays ₹24,999

      │

      ├─ ₹11,250 becomes ads  ──────────►  50-70 LEADS

      └─ ₹13,749 is our margin

TWO KINDS OF LEAD, TWO DIFFERENT OWNERS

CAMPAIGN LEAD (he paid for it)

   → his layout, permanently. We never divert it. Ever.

ORGANIC LEAD (we paid for it — page, website, walk-in, requirement)

   1️⃣  Our own listings first    (24h Tier 1 / 6h Tier 2, only if a match exists)

   2️⃣  Then promoter layouts     → helps them, builds renewal

   3️⃣  Then the agent ladder     → subscription \+ wallet revenue

   4️⃣  "Not now" → nurture       → free lead again in 60 days

**The absolute rule:** a lead carrying a `campaignId` can only ever be matched to that campaign's layout. If a promoter discovers his ₹24,999 generated a buyer who was shown our property instead, we lose him and he tells everyone. In a market this size that is fatal.

**Where agents actually get leads:** buyer requirement posts · unmatched organic leads · enquiries on their own listings · the requirement board. Campaign leads reach an agent **only if that promoter asks us to send them.**

## THE FIVE REVENUE LINES

| Line | Who pays | Why they keep paying |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Promoter subscription** | Layout promoters | Buyers arrive; no other local option |
| **Agent subscription** | Brokers | Exclusive buyers \+ zero commission taken |
| **Wallet top-ups** | Brokers | They want one more buyer, right now |
| **Content & add-ons** | Both | Shoots, reels, featured, boosts |
| **Our own brokerage 2-3%** | Sellers, brokerage-only promoters | We sell their property |

**In-house inventory sources:** owner mandates · brokerage-only promoter listings (free to list, we take 2-3%) · lapsed subscribers' unsold plots · exclusive block deals at 3-4% · resale from past buyers · bank auction plots. Target 20-30 live listings, or the 24-hour hold is pointless.

Promoters ≈ 70% of revenue. **Wallet is the line that grows without limit** — subscriptions are capped by how many brokers exist; wallet is capped only by how many good leads we make.

## WHAT KILLS THIS

1. **Building before selling.** First 10 promoters need WhatsApp and a sheet, not software.  
2. **Spending the advances.** A ₹59,999 quarterly plan is a 90-day obligation. Ring-fence the ad money the day it lands.  
3. **Opening cities too fast.** Two profitable cities beat eight breakeven ones.  
4. **Losing the Instagram page.** Single point of failure — mirror it, back it up, start a second.

**Pivot trigger: fewer than 8 paying promoters on day 90 → stop building a platform, run it as a service agency.** ₹3-4L/month, no engine needed.
