## 5\. PIVOT TRIGGER — DECIDE NOW, WHILE CALM

> **If there are fewer than 8 paying builders on day 90**, stop building a platform and run The Plot One as a service agency: shoot \+ ads per project, no subscriptions, no agent seats, no engine.

That's a solid ₹3–4L/month business requiring almost none of the development work. Writing the trigger down now prevents six more months of building out of momentum.

## 6\. THE FOUR RISKS THAT ACTUALLY KILL THIS

**Building before selling.** The specs are complete, which makes it tempting to build for three months first. The first ten builders don't need software — WhatsApp and the tracker sheet are enough. Sell, then automate what hurts.

**Spending the advances.** A ₹59,999 three-month plan is a 90-day obligation, not revenue. Ring-fence the ad portion the day it lands or month three arrives with commitments and no budget.

**Single-channel dependency.** The Instagram page is the moat and a single point of failure. Second page, YouTube Shorts mirror, owned WhatsApp list, local backups of every reel — start this month, not after a strike.

**Diverting a promoter's paid lead.** The one mistake that ends the business in a town. A lead with a `campaignId` must be structurally incapable of matching any other inventory — enforced in the database, not in policy.
